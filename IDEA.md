# Idea brief · Shopper Inflation Gauge

**Hackathon:** ML Empowerment Build Challenge 4.0  
**URL:** https://ml-build-challenge-4.devpost.com/  
**Deadline:** Wednesday, December 2, 2026, 2:45 AM America/Toronto (Tuesday, December 1, 2026, 11:45 PM Pacific)  
**Build window:** November 1, 2026 through the deadline  
**Solo entry by:** John Khan, Toronto, Ontario

## The problem
Published inflation is weighted to an official basket. Shoppers who spend a larger share on a fast-rising group, such as transportation, live a different 12-month change. That gap is hard to see on a press release.

## The idea
A static page: type monthly spending by major CPI group, apply official Statistics Canada 12-month changes, and read your weighted rate beside the official all-items rate, with the two groups that contribute most.

## Why this fits the challenge
The event asks for a real-world student prototype with a clear problem, a working demo, and sources. Public CPI tables are enough. The page is a statistical weighting tool. It does not need a paid model and does not invent numbers.

## Scope for this build
- Ontario and Canada, eight major groups, September 2026 figures bundled from the official tables
- 2025 official basket weights for comparison
- Labelled demo amounts
- Toronto all-items as context only

## Out of scope
Forecasts, scraped unofficial prices, or filling missing city category series.

## User
A person in Ontario who knows roughly what they spend on food, shelter, transport and the other major groups, and wants to see how that mix compares with the published rate.

## Success
A judge can press the demo button, see a personal rate, see two highlighted groups, and open the Statistics Canada table links.
