What a small model learns from five years of price changes

A ridge regression, trained in your browser, looks only at monthly twelve-month changes for the eight groups and for all-items. It learns how much each group moves the headline rate. Those learned weights are then compared with the official 2025 basket weights, and the model is scored on the last 12 months, which it never saw during training.

This model explains the past. It does not forecast prices. Basket weights change when Statistics Canada updates the basket, so learned weights blend several basket years.
